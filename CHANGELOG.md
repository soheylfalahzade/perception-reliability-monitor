## [0.1.0]

### Added
- Reference-free quality features, detector-health features, logistic reliability model and a hysteretic NORMAL/DEGRADED/FALLBACK state machine.
- Synthetic road scene with five degradations and a camera blackout, classical reference detector, labelled datasets and evaluation.
- Closed-loop SUMO study with four control policies; data-driven animation (`relmon-gif`); static typing and CI.
